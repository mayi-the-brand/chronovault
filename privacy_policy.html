Privacy Policy – ChronoVault

Effective date: 1 January 2025  |  Last updated: 1 January 2025

1. Introduction

ChronoVault ("the App") is a history-ordering quiz game developed for Android devices. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information relating to its users.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

ChronoVault does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personally identifiable information. Specifically, the App does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact details
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to any server
  • Payment information

3. Local Storage Only

The App stores game progress (completed rounds, star ratings, sound preferences) exclusively on the user's own device using Android's SharedPreferences mechanism. This data never leaves the device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

4. Third-Party Services

ChronoVault does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The App does not display advertisements.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Because the App collects no data at all, it is safe for users of all ages.

6. Permissions

The App requests no special Android permissions. It does not require access to the camera, microphone, contacts, location, or internet.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated in the future, the revised policy will be published at the same URL. Continued use of the App after changes constitute acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer via the Google Play Store listing for ChronoVault.

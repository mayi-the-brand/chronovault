LUMITRACE
PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 1 January 2025

The short version: Lumitrace is a single-player offline puzzle game. We do not collect your name, email, location, or any personal information. We do not serve ads. We do not sell data. All game progress is stored locally on your device only.

01

Introduction

Welcome to Lumitrace ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information, if any, is collected when you play Lumitrace and how it is handled. By downloading or playing the Game you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Lumitrace is a locally-run mobile puzzle game. It does not require an account, does not connect to any server, and does not transmit any data over the internet during normal gameplay.

02

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information. Lumitrace does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personally identifying information.

The only data Lumitrace writes is game progress, stored locally on your device:

Level completion status
Star ratings per level
Highest unlocked level
Sound on / off preference
Haptic feedback preference

This data is written using Android's SharedPreferences storage API and never leaves your device.

03

Automatically Collected Data

Lumitrace itself collects no analytics, crash reports, or device identifiers. However, the Google Play Store platform may collect certain technical information (such as crash logs and aggregate install statistics) as part of its standard developer services. This data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and is not accessible to us in an individually identifiable form.

04

Advertising

Lumitrace contains no advertisements. We do not use any ad networks, ad SDKs, or ad-tracking identifiers. No advertising data of any kind is collected.
05

Third-Party Services

Lumitrace does not integrate with third-party analytics services, social networks, payment processors, or cloud services. The Game operates entirely offline once installed.

The only third-party platform involved in the delivery of the Game is the Google Play Store, whose own privacy practices are described at policies.google.com/privacy.

06

Children's Privacy

Lumitrace is rated Everyone (E) and is suitable for all ages, including children under 13.

Because the Game collects no personal information from any user, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar international regulations. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

07

Permissions

Lumitrace requests only the following Android permissions:

VIBRATE — haptic feedback on tile rotation

No camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage, or network access permissions are requested or required.

08

Data Retention & Deletion

All game-progress data is stored locally on your device inside the app's private storage sandbox. You can delete it at any time by:

Settings → Apps → Lumitrace → Clear Data
Uninstalling the app

Neither action requires contacting us. No data is retained on any remote server because no data is ever transmitted off your device.

09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the Game after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

If we ever introduce features that collect personal data, we will obtain appropriate consent before doing so and update this policy accordingly.
